## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Pythology's research agenda represents a comprehensive 10-year vision for advancing systematic trading through the application of advanced mathematics, physics, and computer science to financial markets.

**Current Status:**
- **Phase 1 (Complete):** Core profitable strategies deployed and forward testing
- **Phase 2 (In Progress):** Advanced research prototypes (Projects 1-5)
- **Phase 3 (Planned):** Institutional-grade infrastructure projects (Projects 6-15)

**Research Philosophy:**
We treat markets as complex adaptive systems rather than purely stochastic processes. Our approach combines:
- Econophysics and complexity theory
- Information theory and causality analysis
- Machine learning and statistical modeling
- Microstructure analysis and order flow dynamics

**Resource Requirements:**
Projects 1-5 can be prototyped with current infrastructure. Projects 6-15 require institutional partnerships providing:
- High-frequency tick and Level 2 data
- Significant computing resources (AWS/cloud clusters)
- Research team collaboration
- Production trading infrastructure
